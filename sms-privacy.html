Focus Hive Inc. SMS Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 28, 2026

This SMS Privacy Policy supplements our general Privacy Policy and describes how Focus Hive Inc. ("Focus Hive," "we," "us") handles information in connection with text messaging under our SMS Terms & Conditions.

1. Information We Collect Through SMS

  • Mobile phone number you provide on a form, by text, or to a team member.
  • Consent records: the date, time, and source of your opt-in and any opt-out.
  • Message content exchanged with us, and delivery metadata.

2. How We Use It

  • To send and receive the one-to-one conversational messages you agreed to receive.
  • To honor STOP, START, and HELP requests and keep consent records.
  • To comply with law and carrier requirements.

3. Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers (such as our telecommunications and messaging providers) that deliver messages on our behalf, and where required by law. Mobile numbers and SMS consent are never shared or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

4. Your Choices

  • Opt out: reply STOP to any message; you will receive one confirmation and no further messages from that number.
  • Resubscribe: reply START.
  • Help: reply HELP, or contact us below.

Msg & data rates may apply.

5. Security and Retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect SMS data and retain consent records for as long as required by law and carrier rules.
