Focus Hive Inc. SMS Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 31, 2025

1. Introduction

This SMS Privacy Policy describes how Focus Hive Inc., also known as "Focus Hive" ("we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and shares information about you when you interact with us via Short Message Service (SMS) or text messages. This policy applies specifically to our SMS communication practices.

By sending or receiving SMS messages with Focus Hive, you agree to the collection, use, and disclosure of your information as described in this SMS Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect Through SMS

When you communicate with us via SMS, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Phone Number: We collect your mobile phone number when you send us an SMS message or when our employees initiate an SMS conversation with you.
  • Message Content: We collect the content of the SMS messages exchanged between you and Focus Hive, including any information you voluntarily provide.
  • Metadata: This includes information such as the date, time, and technical details of the SMS messages (e.g., delivery status).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected through SMS for the following purposes:

  • To Communicate With You: To send and receive messages related to our services, your inquiries, customer support, and other transactional or informational purposes as part of a one-to-one conversation initiated by our employees.
  • To Provide and Improve Our Services: To facilitate our services, understand how our SMS communications are used, and improve the effectiveness of our communications.
  • To Manage Opt-Ins and Opt-Outs: To process your requests to opt-in to or opt-out of receiving SMS messages from us, in compliance with applicable regulations (e.g., responding to keywords like START, STOP, HELP).
  • For Compliance and Legal Obligations: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes.

4. Sharing Your Information

Focus Hive Inc. does not sell your personal information collected via SMS. We may share your information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We may share your information with third-party service providers who help us deliver and manage our SMS communications (e.g., telecommunication providers, SMS gateway providers). These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and use it only for the services we have engaged them to perform.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in the good faith belief that such action is necessary to comply with a legal obligation, protect and defend our rights or property, prevent fraud, act in urgent circumstances to protect the personal safety of users of the Service, or protect against legal liability.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, reorganization, bankruptcy, or other similar event, your information may be transferred as part of the transaction, subject to standard confidentiality agreements.

5. Your Choices and Rights

You have choices regarding the SMS messages you receive from us:

  • Opt-Out: You can opt-out of receiving further SMS messages from a specific Focus Hive number at any time by replying "STOP" to any message you receive from that number. After you send the "STOP" message, we will send you an SMS message to confirm that you have been unsubscribed. After this, you will no longer receive SMS messages from that particular number.
  • Opt-In Again: If you wish to join again, reply "START" to that number, and we will resume sending SMS messages to you.
  • Help: If you need assistance or more information regarding our SMS service, reply "HELP" to any message you receive from us, or contact us as described below.

Standard message and data rates may apply to any messages sent or received.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect the information we collect through SMS from loss, theft, misuse, and unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.
